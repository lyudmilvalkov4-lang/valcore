Logistics Process

How a shipment moves.

We source liquid asphalt worldwide and from local refineries, then deliver it to your site based on your specifications and schedule.

  1. 01

    Liquid Asphalt Sourcing

    We can source liquid asphalt from producers around the world and local refineries. If PMA is required, we polymer-modify the material at our plant.

  2. 02

    Quality Verification

    Certificates of analysis are checked against PG specification limits before a shipment is confirmed against your order.

  3. 03

    Logistics Coordination

    We coordinate truck loading windows, transit routing, and delivery timing between the producer or terminal and your dispatch team.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Material arrives at your asphalt base or logistics point on the agreed schedule, with specification and COA documentation in hand.
