Quality

Every shipment checked before it moves.

Each shipment comes with a certificate of analysis (COA) from the producer or terminal. Valcore reviews the results against PG specification limits before confirming the order.

01

DSR

Dynamic Shear Rheometer

DSR testing measures rheological behavior at high and intermediate service temperatures.

It is used to check original binder, RTFO-aged binder, and PAV-aged residue, including G*/sin delta and G*.sin delta results.

02

BBR

Bending Beam Rheometer

BBR testing evaluates low-temperature stiffness and creep response.

The S value and m-value help confirm that the binder meets cold-weather performance requirements.

03

PAV

Pressure Aging Vessel

PAV conditioning simulates long-term binder aging in service.

The aged residue is then tested to verify retained performance after extended pavement exposure.

04

RTFO

Rolling Thin-Film Oven

RTFO testing simulates short-term aging during plant mixing and placement.

The residual binder is checked for mass change and high-temperature shear performance.
